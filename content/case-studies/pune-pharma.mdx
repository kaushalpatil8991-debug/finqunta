## The setup before

Sahyadri Pharma is a regional pharma distributor serving hospitals and retail pharmacies across western Maharashtra. Pre-engagement profile:

- **2,200 SKUs** with batch and expiry tracking (mandatory for pharma)
- **4 warehouses** with regional stock positions
- **₹18 lakhs annual write-off** to expired stock — structurally the biggest controllable cost
- **FDA inspections** consuming 3 full days each because statutory registers (Schedule-H, narcotics) were prepared by hand from Tally reports
- **Batch recalls** were the scariest thing — the last one took 6 hours to trace customer impact

The operations head put it plainly: *"Pharma is a low-margin, high-stakes business. We cannot afford the expiry leakage, and we cannot afford a 6-hour recall."*

## What we deployed

**Phase 1 (weeks 1–3): Pharma vertical pack rollout**
We configured the Pharma vertical pack on their existing TallyPrime Gold installation. The core change: every item moved to batch + expiry tracking with FEFO (first-expiry-first-out) issue rules. Invoices automatically pick the nearest-expiry batch; overrides require manager approval with reason code.

**Phase 2 (weeks 4–5): Expiry alerting + reporting**
Multi-stage expiry alerts were configured: 90-day, 60-day, and 30-day warnings. The 90-day alert triggers a review for movement planning (can we push this stock to higher-turnover customers?). The 60-day alert triggers a price review (can we clear it with a discount?). The 30-day alert triggers a write-off estimation.

**Phase 3 (weeks 6–8): Statutory registers + recall workflow**
Schedule-H and narcotics registers were automated — one-click export in FDA-expected format. We also built a batch-recall workflow: scan the batch number, get customer impact (who received it, when, quantities) in under 30 seconds.

**Phase 4 (ongoing): Monthly reviews**
Monthly review with ops head covering: expiry buckets, FEFO overrides, stock rotation by category, upcoming 90-day expiries. Review is 30 minutes; output is a 2-page action list.

## The outcome

First full year after rollout:

- **Expiry losses**: down from ₹18 L to ₹10.4 L (-42%)
- **90-day review hit rate**: 87% of flagged SKUs cleared before 30-day expiry
- **FDA inspection time**: 3 days → 4 hours average (statutory registers ready on day one)
- **Batch recalls**: two trials run (internal mock), both completed within 3 minutes
- **Operations head stress index** (self-reported): *"Meaningfully lower."*
