## The setup before

Aarambh Foundation is a mid-sized NGO working on education and skill-development in rural Maharashtra. Scale: ₹6.2 Cr annual spend, 9 concurrent projects, 4 funders (2 corporate CSR, 1 state government, 1 FCRA-regulated multilateral), ~30 employees + ~80 field volunteers.

Their Tally was technically clean — all vouchers posted, all banks reconciled — but the accounting structure wasn't built for NGO workflows:

- **Projects mixed on the same ledgers.** Pulling a single project's utilisation required filtering and Excel acrobatics.
- **80G receipts** issued by hand from a Word template, with a 2-week lag on bulk donations.
- **Funder reports** prepared from scratch each quarter, taking a full 3 weeks including iteration on funder-specific format requests.
- **FCRA return** took a full working day each quarter — largely tracing FCRA-fund transactions across commingled ledgers.
- **Statutory audit** came with 4-5 rounds of follow-up from the auditor because utilisation trails were hard to walk.

## What we deployed

**Phase 1 (weeks 1–3): Project-wise fund accounting**
We rebuilt the chart of accounts to support project-wise accounting — every voucher now carries a project tag, every ledger can be viewed at project granularity. Existing vouchers were back-tagged for the current financial year.

**Phase 2 (weeks 4–5): Funder report templates**
Five funder-report templates were built: FCRA, state government, and three corporate-CSR variants. Each draws from Tally directly; quarterly close now produces funder-ready reports at the same time as management accounts.

**Phase 3 (weeks 6–7): Donor workflow automation**
80G receipt generation was automated at donation-time (receipt on the day, not the month). Year-end Form 10BE batch export was configured.

**Phase 4 (week 8): FCRA ring-fencing**
The FCRA bank account and all FCRA-funded projects were ring-fenced in the chart of accounts. FCRA quarterly return now generates in one pass from Tally — auto-populated, ready to review.

**Phase 5 (week 9–10): Training + audit-readiness walk-through**
Training for the accounts and programme teams. We also did a joint walk-through with their statutory auditor to align on the new structure — the auditor was delighted.

## The outcome

Three quarters after rollout:

- **Funder report cycle**: 3 weeks → same-day (for all 4 funders)
- **80G receipts**: generated at donation-time, zero manual effort
- **FCRA quarterly return prep**: 8 hours → 1 hour
- **Statutory auditor follow-ups**: 4-5 rounds → 1 round (clarifying minor items)
- **Programme team visibility**: each project lead now self-serves their own utilisation report weekly

The finance head's comment: *"For the first time, I'm not scared of quarter-end."*

## Stack deployed

- TallyPrime Gold (upgraded from ERP 9 during engagement)
- NGO vertical pack
- Smart Backup++ (FCRA compliance requirement — off-site backup)
- Audit Trail add-on
- 5 custom funder-report templates
