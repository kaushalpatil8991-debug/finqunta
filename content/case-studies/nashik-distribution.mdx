## The setup before

Konkan Traders runs a mid-sized distribution business across Nashik, Pune, and Ahmednagar — three godowns, roughly 45 daily Tally users, ~18,000 SKUs across FMCG and general trade. Before engaging Finquanta, their Tally landscape was:

- **Three separate Tally companies** (one per godown) running on TallyPrime Gold
- **Manual consolidation** every month — a senior accountant emailed data files between branches and merged them in a 4th master Tally
- **Branch-to-HQ discrepancies** found only at month-end, requiring one-off voucher adjustments to explain
- **Commission calculations** for 12 sales reps built in a separate Excel workbook that pulled Tally exports

The accounts head estimated month-end close was consuming 6 full business days, with the last two spent explaining discrepancies to the CEO rather than fixing anything.

## What we deployed

A two-phase rollout over 8 weeks.

**Phase 1 (weeks 1–3): Data synchronisation**
We configured Tally data sync between the three branches and HQ — hourly during business hours, with conflict-resolution rules per voucher type. Branch vouchers now flow to HQ within an hour; HQ changes (mostly master-data updates) flow back to branches.

**Phase 2 (weeks 4–5): Reconciliation Booster + voucher approval flow**
The Reconciliation Booster was installed on all three branches. Weekly Monday email digest now covers branch-vs-HQ reconciliation, bank reco, and GSTR-2A match — exceptions flagged for immediate attention.

We also tightened the voucher-approval flow: credit notes and discount ceilings now route through the branch manager's phone (via our **Transaction Approvals** mobile app) with a 4-hour SLA. This eliminated the "why was this credit issued without my knowledge" pattern that was a frequent cause of month-end adjustments.

**Phase 3 (weeks 6–8): Commission automation + training**
Commission calculations were rebuilt as a Tally TDL that runs nightly; output goes directly to a preconfigured Excel workbook that managers approve from their phones. Commission payout vouchers are posted back to Tally with a single click after approval.

Team training ran across all three locations in weeks 6–7; we stayed on-site for the first month-end after go-live as belt-and-braces.

## The outcome

After three month-end cycles with the new setup:

- **Month-end close time**: down from 6 days to 2 days (-67%)
- **Branch-vs-HQ discrepancies**: 90% caught within 48 hours (vs. month-end)
- **Commission payout cycle**: 3 days → same-day
- **Finance team overtime at month-end**: effectively zero

The accounts head's comment: *"Last month-end, I left on time Friday. That has not happened in seven years."*
