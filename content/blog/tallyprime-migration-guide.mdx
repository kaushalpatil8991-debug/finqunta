## Why migrate at all?

Tally ERP 9 is stable, well-understood, and hundreds of thousands of Indian SMEs still run it happily. But Tally Solutions has moved on: new GST features, e-invoice enhancements, dashboard capabilities, and connected banking ship only on **TallyPrime**. Sooner or later the migration is non-optional.

The good news: the TallyPrime data format is compatible with ERP 9's — the migration is not a re-implementation, it is an upgrade-in-place. The friction is almost entirely human: retraining your team on a rearranged keyboard map.

## What the migration actually involves

At a high level there are four workstreams, all of which can run in parallel:

1. **Data validation.** Confirm your ERP 9 data is clean — balanced books, no orphan masters, no corrupted vouchers. Finquanta does this as a standard pre-migration audit; the fix list is usually short.
2. **TDL port.** Any custom TDL on ERP 9 may need small tweaks for TallyPrime. Most of the time this is a matter of updating screen references; occasionally a wholesale rewrite for features that depended on ERP 9-specific UI.
3. **Training.** TallyPrime re-arranges the keyboard shortcuts and menu layout. The big-bang first hour can feel disorientating. Role-based training (4 hours per role is typical) smooths this out.
4. **Cutover.** A planned weekend cutover with a parallel run for 7–14 days is the safe pattern. Rollback to ERP 9 is always available during the parallel-run window.

## The cutover playbook

A zero-drama cutover typically looks like this:

| Day | Step |
| --- | --- |
| T-14 | Pre-migration audit. Fix any data-consistency issues identified. |
| T-10 | TDL validation + port on a copy of the data. |
| T-7  | First round of role-based training. Assign a TallyPrime champion per team. |
| T-1  | Final ERP 9 backup. |
| T-0 (Sat 18:00) | Convert data to TallyPrime. Install TallyPrime on primary machines. |
| T-0 (Sun 10:00) | Spot-check reports against ERP 9 archive. Sign off cutover. |
| T+1 → T+7 | Parallel-run window. Support channel on WhatsApp for the team. Daily standup. |
| T+14 | Formal sign-off. ERP 9 archived. |

## The training question

The single thing most teams underestimate is the training demand. TallyPrime's "Go To" navigation is strictly better than ERP 9's menu tree — but it is *different*, and muscle memory takes two weeks to rewire.

We recommend investing in **two rounds of training**: one a week *before* cutover (on a sandbox) and one a week *after* (on live data with real vouchers). The second round catches all the "how do I…" questions your team only discovers when they try to do their real job.

## A note on TSS

Tally Software Services — the annual subscription — is what delivers ongoing statutory updates. Most ERP 9 installations in the wild have let TSS lapse. **Renew TSS during the migration**, not after; this gets you the latest TallyPrime build and every subsequent patch as it ships.
