## The AMC market

Tally AMCs are easy to buy — every Tally partner offers them. They are harder to buy *well*. The difference between a good AMC partnership and a mediocre one typically shows up on day 45, when something breaks at 14:30 on a Tuesday and you find out exactly how responsive your partner is.

Here are the six questions we advise customers to ask *every* potential AMC partner — including us.

## 1. What is your response time SLA, in writing?

"Best efforts" is not an SLA. A real SLA commits to a response time in hours (often tiered by severity), in writing, with a measurement and escalation protocol. If your candidate AMC partner cannot put a number on the contract, they do not have an operational SLA — they have a vibe.

## 2. Will I have a named engineer?

"Someone from our team will pick up" means you will get a different person every call, repeat your story every time, and never build the familiarity that makes support fast. A named engineer — with a named backup for holidays — is non-negotiable.

## 3. Do you do onboarding audits before cover starts?

If the partner agrees to a fixed-fee AMC *without* doing an onboarding audit first, they have priced it on average and you are either going to overpay (if your setup is simple) or they are going to lose money and cut corners (if your setup is complex). An onboarding audit aligns expectations and identifies issues before they become Tuesday-at-14:30 incidents.

## 4. What is your escalation path?

Who owns the account if an issue goes critical? Who do you call if the named engineer is unresponsive? A real AMC partner has at least two layers of escalation — engineer → senior engineer → leadership — with named people and direct lines.

## 5. How do you handle statutory updates?

When GST law changes, when e-invoice thresholds move, when a TallyPrime patch drops — who makes sure you have the right version installed in time? A good partner owns this proactively. A mediocre one waits for you to ask.

## 6. Can I cancel mid-term?

A confident partner will let you cancel any time with 30 days' written notice, refunding unused cover less a modest administrative charge. A partner that locks you in for a year is confident only in their sales pipeline, not their retention.

## What to ignore

- **Client logos on their website.** Impressive client lists mean nothing about how you will be treated. What matters is how they treat their smallest customers.
- **Years in business.** New partners with ex-Tally engineers can be superb. Old partners coasting on reputation can be mediocre. Test on the six questions above.
- **Office location.** With modern remote support, the partner's office matters less than their response time. Pune-based partners routinely support Mumbai clients well — and vice versa.

## The conversation to have

Our opening call with every potential AMC client covers:

1. Your setup (users, branches, TallyPrime or ERP 9, TDL list)
2. Your current pain points ("what broke last month?")
3. Your desired SLA level
4. A live walk-through of our SLA matrix and escalation doc
5. A written quote the same day

If your current (or prospective) partner does not run this structure, you are signing a vibe.
