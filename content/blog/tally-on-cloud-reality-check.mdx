## The pitch and the truth

The Tally on Cloud pitch is clean: your TallyPrime, hosted on the internet, accessible from any device, backed up automatically, with 99.9% uptime. For a lot of SMEs this is simply correct and they should move. For some it is not — and forcing the fit creates friction for years.

This post is how we help customers decide, based on the questions that actually matter.

## Question 1: Where are your users?

Tally on Cloud shines when users are distributed — branches, remote sales reps, a founder on the road. If your entire team sits in one office at desks with the same Tally all day, the business case for cloud gets thinner.

## Question 2: How big is your Tally data?

For companies with sub-5 GB Tally data, cloud is basically free (from a data-size perspective). Above 20 GB, hosting costs rise noticeably; above 50 GB, the monthly bill becomes a real line item. Keep this in mind when sizing your cloud vs on-prem calculus.

## Question 3: Do you use custom TDL?

Most TDL works fine on cloud, but some of it (especially file-system-dependent or hardware-dependent TDL) needs either adaptation or a dedicated virtual machine. Audit the TDL list before committing.

## Question 4: What is your internet situation?

Cloud Tally is only as reliable as your internet. Two lines (one primary, one failover), ideally from different providers, is the minimum we advise. Retailers with intermittent internet should think twice — a POS that locks up when the internet drops is a bad day.

## Question 5: What is your current IT team doing?

If you have a capable IT person already running your Tally server, cloud saves them 10% of their time. If you are the founder playing IT on weekends, cloud saves you every weekend.

## The cases where cloud is obvious

- Multi-branch setup with branches outside the HQ city
- Distributed sales team using Tally from the road
- Founder / CFO needing weekend access from home
- No internal IT — anything server-shaped is a burden
- Currently running TallyPrime Server but want to avoid a hardware refresh cycle

## The cases where on-prem is still the right call

- Single-location retail or manufacturing with all users inside
- Sub-10-user team that is happy with a PC under a desk
- Customers with genuine data-sovereignty or compliance constraints that forbid external hosting
- Businesses on very thin margins where even ₹2,000–4,000/month cloud cost matters

## The hybrid middle

There is also a middle path — Tally on Cloud for the HQ team + branch offices, plus a lightweight on-prem read-replica for offline-critical workflows (pharma dispatch, factory shop floor). We've set these up a handful of times; they work, but they are bespoke and you should budget for the setup effort.

## Decision heuristic

If you answered yes to 3+ of Questions 1-5 pointing to cloud, it is probably right for you. If you answered "no" to all 5, on-prem is still the right call. Everything in between deserves a 30-minute scoping conversation.
