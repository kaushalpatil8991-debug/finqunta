## The catalogue is not a checklist

The Tally add-on catalogue runs to hundreds of TDLs across security, automation, productivity, compliance, and reporting. For a first-time buyer, the natural instinct is to install everything that sounds useful. This is the wrong instinct.

Each add-on is a small piece of commitment: a module to maintain, a potential source of upgrade friction, a line in the TDL inventory. The right number of add-ons for most SMEs is **two or three** — the ones that solve your most painful daily friction. Not thirty.

## A quick triage

Rank each candidate add-on against three questions:

1. **Who uses it daily?** If no single person opens it every week, skip it.
2. **What does it replace?** A good add-on replaces a worse current state (Excel, a side system, a manual process). A bad add-on is net-additive.
3. **Can you describe the time saved in minutes per week?** If you can't, you haven't found the pain yet.

## The ones to skip (for most SMEs)

- **Bespoke dashboard TDLs**: If you are buying a dashboard add-on, buy our [MIS Booster](/tally-erp-9-solution-boosters/mis) instead — it bundles what you probably want.
- **Single-feature productivity add-ons**: One-trick TDLs that solve a keyboard shortcut you could learn. Worth it only if you have 20+ users at the counter.
- **Industry-generic packs**: If you are looking at a 'complete pack for retail', look at our [vertical solutions](/tally-erp-9-vertical-solutions) instead — those are tuned for specific industries and much better integrated.
